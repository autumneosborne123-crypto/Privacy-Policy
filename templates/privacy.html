1. Information We Collect

{{ branding }} ("we", "us", or "our") collects minimal data necessary to provide its services. This includes:

  • Discord User IDs: To track leveling progress, economy balances, and achievement history.
  • Guild IDs and Settings: To store your server configurations, such as moderation thresholds and logging channels.
  • Message Content: Only when necessary for specific features (e.g., leveling, moderation) and only processed in real-time. We do not store your private messages.

2. How We Use Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Provide and maintain the bot's features (Leveling, Economy, Security).
  • Allow server administrators to manage their server settings through the dashboard.
  • Improve bot performance and troubleshoot technical issues.

3. Data Retention

Data is stored as long as the bot is present in your server or until you request its deletion. If you leave a server or the bot is removed, your user-specific data (like XP and balance) may persist in our database to allow you to resume if you return, unless a deletion request is made.

4. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data. However, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or wish to request data deletion, please join our Support Server.
