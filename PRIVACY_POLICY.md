# Privacy Policy for FlowerBot

**Last Updated: August 2, 2026**

This Privacy Policy describes how FlowerBot ("the Bot", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and shares information when you use the Bot in a Discord server.

By adding FlowerBot to your server or using its features, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

## 1. Information We Collect

We collect minimal data necessary to provide and maintain the Bot's functionality. This includes:

### A. Personal Data (via Discord)
*   **Discord User IDs:** A unique identifier provided by Discord to track your leveling progress (XP), economy balances (Flower Coins), achievement history, and music playlists.
*   **Username and Avatar:** Used for display purposes within the Bot's commands. We do not store historical usernames or avatars; we fetch the current ones via the Discord API.

### B. Server (Guild) Data
*   **Guild IDs:** Unique identifiers for servers where the Bot is present to store server-specific configurations.
*   **Settings:** Information such as logging channel IDs, welcome/goodbye messages, role reward configurations, and disabled modules.
*   **Moderation Logs:** If using moderation features, we may store warning history, including the user ID of the moderator and the reason provided.

### C. Usage and Activity Data
*   **Message Metadata:** We track the number of messages sent and voice channel activity (duration) to calculate XP and leveling statistics.
*   **Command Usage:** We may log command execution (without arguments) for debugging and performance optimization purposes.
*   **Media Feeds:** URLs of media shared in designated "media feed" channels if configured by server administrators.

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:
*   **Functionality:** To operate leveling, economy, adventure (animals/inventory), and music features.
*   **Moderation:** To provide server administrators with tools to manage their community (e.g., warning systems).
*   **Support:** To troubleshoot technical issues and respond to user inquiries.

## 3. Data Storage and Retention

*   **Storage:** Data is stored in a secure SQLite database.
*   **Retention:** We retain your data as long as you are active in a server where the Bot is present. 
*   **Persistent Data:** Leveling and economy data may persist after you leave a server to allow you to resume progress if you return.
*   **Deletion:** Server administrators can reset server data at any time. Individual users can request data deletion by contacting us (see Section 7).

## 4. Third-Party Services

FlowerBot operates on the **Discord** platform. Your use of the Bot is also subject to [Discord's Privacy Policy](https://discord.com/privacy) and [Terms of Service](https://discord.com/terms).

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your data to outside parties.

## 5. Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data. However, please remember that no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure.

## 6. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your data:
*   The right to access the data we hold about you.
*   The right to request the deletion of your data.
*   The right to object to the processing of your data.

To exercise these rights, please join our support server.
